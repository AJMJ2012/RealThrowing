Brings back the glory of the Throwing class by converting various "thrown" weapons to use the ol' Throwing damage type.
Flails and Yoyos are also included, but can be turned off separately.

Due to the lack of throwing armor and prefixes in vanilla, all ranged and melee stats affect melee weapons.
This also includes melee effects such as flasks, and ranged ammo saving.

Split Mod's Raquets are also converted (weird choice), but they can also be turned off separately.
